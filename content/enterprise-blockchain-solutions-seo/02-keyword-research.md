# 02 — Keyword Research

**Research date:** 26 November 2026

## Data availability: read first

No keyword tool (Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, Search Console) was available during this research. **Search volume and keyword difficulty are therefore marked "Unavailable" throughout.** We have not estimated or invented them.

What the classifications below *are* based on:
- Live US search results for the key queries (who ranks and what page types appear) → **intent**
- Competitor heading and topic analysis → **relevance and section fit**
- Buyer-journey reasoning → **priority**

**Recommended next step for the ClickMasters team:** export volume and KD for this list from Keyword Planner or Semrush, and pull current query data for this URL from Google Search Console. Then revisit the priorities. The column structure below is ready for that data.

Priority key: **P1** = target in title/H1/H2 and body. **P2** = target in H2/H3 and body. **P3** = natural mention or FAQ only.

## 1. Primary keyword

**`enterprise blockchain solutions`**

Why this one:
- It matches the existing URL slug (`/service/enterprise-blockchain-solutions`) and the current page title, so continuity is preserved.
- It's the broadest commercial-and-informational term for this service.
- Its results mix educational and vendor pages (IBM, Visa, Oracle), so a page combining explainer depth with services can satisfy both intents.

Paired closest variant, used in the SEO title: **`enterprise blockchain development`**. Its results are clearly commercial, with agency pages dominating.

## 4. Final keyword set for the page

- **Primary:** enterprise blockchain solutions
- **Close variants (P1):** enterprise blockchain development, enterprise blockchain development company, enterprise blockchain development services
- **Secondary (P2):** permissioned / private / consortium blockchain development, enterprise blockchain integration, enterprise blockchain implementation, enterprise blockchain security, Hyperledger Fabric, Hyperledger Besu, blockchain architecture, ERP integration
- **Semantic and entity support (P3):** distributed ledger, shared ledger, smart contracts, chaincode, private data collections, channels, QBFT, MSP, certificate authority, endorsement policy, notary, R3 Corda, tokenization, interoperability, Hyperledger FireFly, Cacti, Kubernetes, infrastructure as code, HSM, audit trail, reconciliation, traceability

**Density guidance:** the exact primary phrase appears in the title, H1, the first H2, one FAQ question, the final CTA H2 and a few body mentions (7 exact-match uses in roughly 5,500 words, as measured in the final draft). Variants carry the rest. Don't add more exact-match repetitions.

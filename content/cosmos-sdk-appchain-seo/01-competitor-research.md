### What the SERP tells us
1. **Chainscore Labs is the long-tail incumbent.** It publishes dozens of templated Cosmos pages (one per industry × service) plus a blog cluster on appchain cost and sovereignty. It wins breadth, not depth.
2. **The "company" SERP is soft.** Several ranking URLs are stale or broken (LeewayHertz now serves AI content; Nadcab 404s; Merehead redirects). A strong, current page can take positions here.
3. **"Application-specific blockchain development" and "custom Cosmos blockchain development" have no specialist service page.** Real gap.
4. **Informational results are owned by docs.cosmos.network and Coinbase.** A commercial page will not outrank them on "what is…" queries. It should answer those questions briefly to satisfy buyers, while targeting the commercial queries.
5. Antier, Innowise, Boosty Labs, LimeChain, SoluLab, Suffescom, Blockchain App Factory and Maticz did **not** appear for any Cosmos query tested. Antier has a `/cosmos-sdk/` page but it was not ranking in our checks.

## 4. Synthesis

### 4a. What the strongest pages do well (learn from; do not copy)
- **Transparent scoping** (Chainscore): tier tables by module count, audit passes and support length. Buyers value budget orientation.
- **De-risking narrative** (Rapid Innovation): naming failure modes (appchain chosen too early, relayer failures) builds credibility.
- **"Fit assessment" first step** (Rapid Innovation): positions the agency as an advisor, not a vendor.
- **Engineering vocabulary** (Webisoft): submessages, migrations, admin keys. Signals real hands-on work.
- **Architecture explainer** (Techfyte): ABCI, multistore, keepers. Satisfies technical evaluators.
- **Comparison tables** (Chainscore C2, Rapid Innovation): appchain vs L1 vs smart contract.

### 4b. Common weaknesses → opportunities
1. **Outdated terminology:** "Tendermint Core", Ethermint, gaiacli, SDK v0.47, CosmWasm 1.5. **Opportunity:** a page that is visibly current (CometBFT, SDK v0.53+, ibc-go v10, IBC v2, Cosmos EVM, CosmWasm 2.x).
2. **Incorrect IBC claims:** Hub as required relay, "zero trust", native IBC to Ethereum. **Opportunity:** explain trust assumptions precisely; this is a trust signal to technical buyers.
3. **ICQ absent everywhere; ICA nearly absent.** **Opportunity:** explain both in business terms.
4. **No full decision framework** across smart contract vs rollup vs sovereign appchain vs shared security. **Opportunity:** the page's signature section.
5. **Shared security is missing.** **Opportunity:** honest treatment of Interchain Security / partial set security status.
6. **Validator bootstrapping, genesis ceremony, gentx, sentry/remote-signer architecture are missing.** **Opportunity:** a launch playbook section.
7. **Upgrades (x/upgrade, Cosmovisor, store migrations) are missing.** **Opportunity:** a "life after mainnet" section, which matters for maintenance retainers.
8. **Testing methodology is generic.** **Opportunity:** simulation, interchaintest, invariant checks, fuzzing.
9. **Unsupported performance claims** ("10k TPS", "aBFT", "sub-second"). **Opportunity:** say finality is deterministic and block time is configurable, without inventing numbers.
10. **No verifiable Cosmos case studies anywhere.** **Opportunity:** if ClickMasters can supply even one real Cosmos/Go/Rust project, it beats every competitor on evidence. If not, offer architecture samples or a paid discovery deliverable instead of fake logos.

- What is Cosmos SDK / Cosmos blockchain development?
- Can Cosmos SDK be used to build an application-specific blockchain?
- Should we build an appchain or deploy a smart contract elsewhere?
- What programming languages are used?
- How does IBC help? Can you integrate with Ethereum / non-Cosmos chains?
- Can Cosmos support private or enterprise chains?
- Difference between CosmWasm and Cosmos SDK? CosmWasm vs Solidity?
- Can contracts / the chain be upgraded after launch, and who controls that?
- How much does it cost / what drives cost? How is pricing structured?
- How long does it take to launch?
- Do you handle validator set design and launch coordination?
- Can you help with tokenomics and chain parameters?
- How secure is it / what is your audit process?
- What happens after launch?
- Do we own the code and IP?
- What deliverables do we receive?
- Can you migrate an existing product to Cosmos SDK?
- What chains have you built?

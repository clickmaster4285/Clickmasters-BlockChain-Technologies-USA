## Data integrity statement

**No search volume, keyword difficulty or CPC figures appear in this document.** A keyword-data connector (Ubersuggest) is attached to this workspace but wasn't authorised in this session, so it couldn't be queried. Rather than estimate numbers, every keyword below is classified with **evidence we could observe directly**:

- **SERP evidence:** what kind of pages appeared in live search results for the query (vendor service pages, directories or guides).
- **Competitor evidence:** how many of the 11 analysed competitor pages use the phrase in an H1, H2 or H3 (see competitor-research.md).
- **Site-fit:** whether ClickMasters already has a dedicated page for the topic. Topics with their own page are *supporting* keywords here, not targets, to avoid cannibalisation.

**Before you finalise priorities**, pull volume and difficulty for the list below from Ubersuggest (authorise it in claude.ai connector settings), Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs or Semrush. If the target URL already gets impressions, export Search Console queries for it as well.

Competition labels below are qualitative. **High** means the results are full of large vendors and directories with heavy content. **Medium** means a mix of vendors and guides. **Lower** means fewer exact-match commercial pages.

## 4. Commercial-intent modifiers to use naturally

"services", "company", "partner", "team", "build", "hire", "cost", "custom", "end-to-end", "for enterprises", "for startups", "for fintech".

Directory-style terms like **"top" and "best"** are dominated by Clutch, DesignRush and Upwork lists. Don't chase them on a single vendor page.

Use these where they help explain something, not as a checklist.

**Business entities:** tokenization, real-world assets (RWA), supply chain traceability, audit trail, reconciliation, settlement, provenance, digital identity, loyalty programmes.
